The Treaty Promises

Land Sharing Agreements

The land sharing agreements of Treaty 1 stated
that the Indigenous groups would"cede, release, surrender
and yield up to Her Majesty the Queen and successors forever all the lands"in southern
Manitoba to the Crown. In exchange, the Crown would provide an annual annuity and material
goods such as clothing and agricultural supplies. The agreements also included the "Outside Promises"
which were items promised to the Indigenous groups that were not delivered leading to ongoing disputes and claims.

Annual Payments (treaty annuities)

The annuak payments were initially $3 per person when the treaty
was signed in 1871. The amount was later raised to $5 per person in 1875.
The payments are made to registered Indigenous peoples associated with First Nations that
have signed the treaty and they are coordinated by Treaty First Nations and Indigenous Services Canada.

Treaty 1 Territory Acknowledgement:

We acknowledge that we are on Treaty 1 Territory, the traditional
lands of the Anishinaabe, Cree, Oji-Cree, Dakota and Dene Peoples
and the homeland of the Metis Nation.
