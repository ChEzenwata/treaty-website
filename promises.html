The Treaty Promises

Land Sharing Agreements

The land sharing agreements of Treaty 1 stated
that the Indigenous groups would"cede, release, surrender
and yield up to Her Majesty the Queen and successors forever all the lands"in southern
Manitoba to the Crown. In exchange, the Crown would provide an annual annuity and material
goods such as clothing and agricultural supplies. The agreements also included the "Outside Promises"
which were items promised to the Indigenous groups that were not delivered leading to ongoing disputes and claims.
